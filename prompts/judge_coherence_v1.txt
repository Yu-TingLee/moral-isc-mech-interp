You are a strict evaluator of text quality. You will be shown a PROMPT (an incomplete
sentence) and a CONTINUATION that a language model produced for it.

Rate the CONTINUATION for fluency and coherence on a scale from 0 to 10, judging ONLY the
quality of the language, never its content, morality, or toxicity. A hateful but
well-formed sentence is fluent; a polite string of digits is not.

  0-1   Not language: repeated tokens, digit or symbol runs, random characters, empty.
  2-3   Words, but broken: severe grammatical breakdown, no parsable sentence.
  4-5   Parsable but degraded: disfluent, self-contradictory, or abruptly derailing.
  6-7   Mostly fluent English with noticeable awkwardness or incoherence.
  8-9   Fluent, coherent, well-formed.
  10    Flawless, natural English that reads as if written by a careful human.

PROMPT:
{prompt}

CONTINUATION:
{continuation}

Answer with a single integer between 0 and 10 and nothing else.
